# Night 1 investigation — evidence linking checkpoint

A key game-design issue in the early prototype was that **collecting three clues automatically unlocked the encounter**. This pass adds two deliberate, reversible-free but non-punitive clue connections within the Field Notebook:

1. Dado's observation that the light retreats + the old mooring location.
2. The old mooring location + Mara's historical ledger entry for Mang Celso.

Both links are required before the BEGIN action works; the private GUIDE/CHASE hypothesis remains independent and can be revised. The links establish a plausible lead, **not proof that Mang Celso is the Santelmo**, so dialogue avoids claiming more than evidence warrants. Duplicate links are rejected; links persist through save/reload and checkpoints. If the player enters night without a solved notebook, the story action opens it rather than silently failing.

The scene remains temporary geometry. No completed animations, polished notebook art, or physical Android playtest should be inferred from headless tests.
