# Night 6 — What the Water Took (River's Unfinished Memory)

A historical climax, **not** a boss-health battle. The river manifestation is an original, transient environmental memory, not souls of flood victims or a servant of the Ancient Keeper.

Story phases: four independent flood records → reconstruction of the stopped official second rescue → route of the independent returning boat → identity of Aling Sabel's husband corroborated by a boat mark and independent survivor account → private Sabel revelation / Lola confession → prepare a safe crossing → three telegraphed, nonfatal river current challenges → save the evidence and aftermath. Dado testimony optional; privacy never penalized. Wrong hypotheses are private and revisable. Rescuer is separate from Santelmo's boatman. The second rescue was genuinely hazardous AND unequally cancelled; the independent return does not prove an official crossing was safe.

Nights 1–6 are one connected world; Night 5 farm checkpoint leads physically to Night 6's hill-to-river path after canonical Night 5 completion. Night 7 remains locked until a playable scene exists. Use scripts/ui/shared_hud.gd and its Night 1 fixed thumb-control grammar without modifications.

Android development requires Godot and emulator checks; never present green headless script tests as a substitute for visual or physical-device testing.
