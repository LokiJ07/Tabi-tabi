# G4 — In-world Santelmo guidance

Night 1 Resolve now requires **a player action in the actual 3D world**. Previously, clicking the REMEMBER button immediately ended phase 3 regardless of movement, position or lantern aim.

- After the reflection puzzle, a visible **green landing ring** appears at the safe landing (on the village side of the river fence).
- Stand within four world units of the ring, hold FOCUS with the right thumb, and aim **toward the landing, away from Santelmo** for about three seconds.
- Guiding only succeeds with Mang Celso's referenced clue, a calm enough Santelmo and an aligned beam. Wrong aiming or walking away slowly loses progress; the ring does not require precision tapping.
- The glowing Santelmo moves toward the safe landing and changes from frightened orange toward soft gold as guidance progresses.
- REMEMBER becomes available after complete world-space guidance. DRIVE AWAY with a prepared bell and safe PARTIAL/RETREAT remain accessible throughout.
- Progress persists across offline save/reload and checkpoint retry without duplicate results.
- Both river reflection markers are now held at stable global positions while the creature drifts, preventing them from accidentally following Santelmo during startled retreat.
- Narrative and full main-scene integration tests cover the new guiding requirements and in-world target.

**Status**: A real gameplay interaction, but not a finished cinematic, animation performance or verified Android UX. Physical-thumb testing is still required before G4/G6 signoff.
